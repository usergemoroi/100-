## 💵 Revenue Streams (Detailed Breakdown)

### 1. GitHub Sponsors 👑 (60% of revenue)

**Why GitHub Sponsors is #1:**
- ✅ Built into GitHub (where devs already are)
- ✅ 0% fees for first $5k/year
- ✅ Low fees after (3% + payment processing)
- ✅ Shows "Sponsor" button on your repo
- ✅ Integrates with commit history

**Tiers:**

| Tier | Price | What They Get | Expected Conversion |
|------|-------|---------------|---------------------|
| ☕ Supporter | $5/mo | Name in SPONSORS.md, warm fuzzy feeling | 40% of sponsors |
| 💎 Premium Access | $15/mo | Premium themes, early access, exclusive Discord | 45% of sponsors |
| 🔥 Custom Color | $50/mo | Everything above + custom accent color in next theme | 12% of sponsors |
| 👑 Custom Theme | $100/mo | Everything above + personal theme made for you | 3% of sponsors |

**Expected numbers (Month 6):**
- 150 x $5 = $750
- 180 x $15 = $2,700  
- 50 x $50 = $2,500
- 20 x $100 = $2,000
- **Total: $5,950/mo**

**How to promote:**
1. Add to README: "❤️ Love this? Sponsor for $5/mo to support development"
2. Add to SPONSORS.md: Public thank-you to all sponsors
3. Tweet monthly: "52 sponsors now! 🎉 Thank you [tag some of them]"
4. Email users: "If this saved you 1 hour, it's worth $50. Consider $5/mo?"

## 🚨 Common Mistakes (Avoid These)

### ❌ Mistake 1: "I'll add premium later"
**Fix:** Launch with premium from Day 1. Easier to convert early users.

### ❌ Mistake 2: "I don't want to be salesy"
**Fix:** Asking for support ≠ being pushy. Devs pay for tools they value.

### ❌ Mistake 3: "Free should be limited"
**Fix:** Free should be GREAT. Premium should be AMAZING. Don't cripple free tier.

### ❌ Mistake 4: "I'll wait until I have 10k users"
**Fix:** Start monetizing at 100 users. Test pricing, learn what people want.

### ❌ Mistake 5: "One revenue stream is enough"
**Fix:** Diversify. GitHub Sponsors + Patreon + Custom themes = stable income.
